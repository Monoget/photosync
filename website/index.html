Features

Backup that takes care of itself

Pair once. From then on, every new photo finds its way to your PC — quietly, safely, and only over your own network.

Automatic backups

Hourly background sync on Wi-Fi — battery-aware, and it never touches mobile data. Open the app and hit Backup Now whenever you like.

Incremental sync

Only new photos travel. Your phone and PC reconcile what's already backed up, so a full gallery check takes seconds, not hours.

Resumable transfers

Walked out of range mid-photo? Transfers pick up exactly where they stopped — from the same byte, verified end to end.

Private pairing

Your phone and PC meet with a one-time 6-digit code, then trust each other's certificate fingerprint. Nobody else on the network gets in.

An organized library

Photos land in tidy 2026\09 month folders with their original names. Duplicates are detected by content and never stored twice.

Runs quietly

The Windows app lives in your system tray, starts with Windows if you want it to, and comes in light and dark themes.

How it works

Paired in under a minute

Three steps, once. After that, PixSynq works without you.

Install both apps

Unzip PixSynq on your Windows PC and pick a backup folder. Install the app on your Android phone. Join them to the same Wi-Fi.

✓ D:\Pictures\PixSynq
backup destination set

Pair with a code

Your phone finds the PC on the network by itself. Type the 6-digit code shown on your PC screen — it works once, then the devices trust each other for good.

Photos flow home

New photos back up automatically whenever both devices share the Wi-Fi. Watch it happen in the dashboard — or don't. It doesn't need you.
